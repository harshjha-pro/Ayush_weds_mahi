# Go-to-Market & Sales Plan — BFI Marwari Invitations

Built on the business plan (narrow Marwari focus, vendor partnerships first, proof before paid ads) and the features already in the app.

## 1. Positioning
**"Wedding invitations, the Marwari way."**
The only digital invitation studio that specialises in Marwari weddings: rituals (Ganesh puja, Mayra, Mahila sangeet), family names and blessings written correctly, royal Rajasthani design, and a private page where the family tracks everything.

Against alternatives:
| Alternative | Their weakness | BFI answer |
|---|---|---|
| Printed cards | Expensive to post, no RSVP, NRI guests miss out | Instant WhatsApp sharing + QR on printed cards |
| Free template apps | Generic, Western, wrong wording | Marwari-specific designs and wording, checked by humans |
| Freelance designers on Instagram | Unreliable timelines, scattered WhatsApp revisions | Fixed packages, tracked revisions, on-time QA |

## 2. Ideal customers
1. **Marwari families in Jaipur, Jodhpur, Kolkata, Mumbai, Surat, Delhi, Bengaluru** with a wedding 1–4 months away.
2. **NRI Marwari families** (Dubai, Singapore, US, UK) planning a wedding in India.
3. **Repeat families**: siblings' weddings, engagements, receptions, anniversaries (multi-project accounts already supported).

## 3. Offer & pricing
| Package | Price | Who it's for | Role in funnel |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | ₹4,999 | Simple, quick, first-time trust | Entry offer, partner trades |
| **Premium** | ₹11,999 | Most families | Main revenue (target 60% of orders) |
| Luxury / Bespoke | ₹29,999 | Big weddings, NRIs | Margin & portfolio |

Add-ons (₹999–2,499): save-the-date, extra function page, itinerary, travel guide, thank-you page. Upsell at the quote stage and again 3 weeks before the wedding.

**Launch pricing (first 10 orders):** keep list prices visible; give a "founding families" ₹1,000 off Premium in exchange for a review + showcase consent. Avoid deep discounts that anchor low.

**Scarcity (honest):** the capacity indicator ("3 of 5 slots this week") is real — set it to actual studio capacity.

## 4. Channels (in order of priority)

### 4.1 Vendor & affiliate partners (primary)
- **Who:** wedding planners, photographers/videographers, MUAs, mehendi artists, venues/banquets, Marwari content creators.
- **Offer:** 15% (Basic/Premium) / 20% (Luxury) commission on completed paid orders, or a one-time trade (free/discounted invites for their next 3 clients, with credit).
- **Process in the app:** Outreach CRM (Invited → In discussion → Agreed), referral code + link, marketing kit, commission tracker.
- **Target:** 30 vendors pitched in month 1 → 10 active partners by day 90.
- **Pitch script (WhatsApp):** "Namaste {name} 🙏 I'm {you} from BFI — we make Marwari wedding invitation websites. For your next 3 couples we'll make their invitation free, with credit to you on it. After that you earn 15–20% on every order you send. Can I share a sample?"

### 4.2 Direct outreach (client management team)
- Wedding expos, community associations (Marwari sammelans, Maheshwari/Agarwal samaj events), society WhatsApp groups (with permission), temples and dharamshalas that host weddings.
- Leads logged in **Direct Outreach**; team earns 15%/20% on direct orders.
- Follow-up rhythm: call within 2 hours → sample + price in 24h → follow-up day 3 and day 7.

### 4.3 Instagram & Pinterest (organic)
- 3 Reels/week: invitation reveal on a phone, "before/after wording fix", ritual explainers (Mayra, Mahila sangeet), behind the scenes.
- Pinterest boards per design and ritual; every pin links to its design page.
- Only consent-cleared projects (enforced by the app's campaign calendar).

### 4.4 SEO (website)
Target pages: "Marwari wedding invitation", "Marwari wedding card wording", "Mayra invitation", "Rajasthani wedding invitation online", "digital wedding invitation Jaipur/Kolkata/Mumbai". One guide per week for 12 weeks. Track in the SEO tracker.

### 4.5 Referrals from families
After delivery: review request (automated) + personal referral code ("friends get priority booking; you get a thank-you gift"). Target 20% of orders from past clients by month 6.

### 4.6 Paid ads (later)
Only after 10 delivered organic orders (gate already in the app). Start with Instagram Reels boosts to lookalikes of engaged followers, ₹300–500/day, measure CAC in Lead Source & CAC.

## 6. Unit economics (per order, current settings)
| | Basic direct | Premium direct | Premium via vendor | Luxury via vendor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Price | 4,999 | 11,999 | 11,999 | 29,999 |
| Team commission (CM + designer) | 1,500 (30%) | 3,600 (30%) | 2,400 (20%) | 9,000 (30%) |
| Vendor commission | — | — | 1,800 (15%) | 6,000 (20%) |
| Hosting + payment fees (~2%) | ~400 | ~540 | ~540 | ~900 |
| **Left for design hours, QA, overheads, profit** | ~3,100 | ~7,860 | ~7,260 | ~14,100 |

⚠️ **Decision needed:** the app also charges designer *hours* (₹600/h) as a cost. If designers are paid by commission, remove the hourly cost for in-house designers (or vice versa) to avoid paying twice. On Basic via a vendor, 35% goes to commissions — consider making Basic team commission lower or Basic a partner-trade product.
